Adding the widget

Make a list in the app. Then long-press your home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search for "Cute Reminders" and pick the small or medium size.

Designs and purchases

Every new user gets 5 designs free, picked at random. Any other design is a one-time $0.99 unlock, or you can get every design (including new ones) for $14.99. No subscriptions and no ads.

Got a new phone or reinstalled? Tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Designs screen.
