Cute Reminders Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Cute Reminders is a small app made by Scott DiBenedetto, with art by Jo (@jojodrawss). This policy explains what the app keeps, what it sends, and why. The short version is that your reminders never leave your phone. The app sends only a little anonymous information to help us understand which designs people like, and to keep track of purchases.

Your reminders stay on your phone

Everything you type stays on your iPhone: list names, reminders, which items you've checked off, start and stop times, fonts and themes. It's saved on the device so the app and its home screen widget can show it. We don't have a server that receives it, and we can't see it.

If you delete the app, your reminders are deleted with it.

No account, no ads, no tracking

  • You don't need to sign up or log in.
  • There are no ads.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your information with data brokers or advertisers.

Anonymous usage analytics (Amplitude)

We use Amplitude to count how the app gets used, so we know which designs people love and what Jo should draw next. The app sends events like these:

  • a design was picked for a list, or which designs are on your lists that day
  • an unlock screen was opened
  • a design or the "every design" bundle was bought
  • restore purchases was tapped
  • a Cute Reminders widget is on the home screen

These events include the design's name, such as "chefcat", plus whether it was one of your free designs. They never include what you write in your reminders or list names.

Amplitude gives your device a random ID so events from the same phone can be counted together. It also receives basic technical details such as your device model, iOS version, app version and language. We've turned off IP address and city collection, and turned off Amplitude's automatic tracking, so only the events listed above are sent. This information isn't tied to your name, email or Apple ID.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

All payments go through Apple's App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple ID.

We use RevenueCat to keep a record of purchases, so we can see which designs sell and help if a purchase needs restoring. RevenueCat receives which products were bought and when, along with an anonymous ID it creates for the app and basic device details. It doesn't receive your reminders.

Children

Cute Reminders is for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Nothing the app sends identifies who you are.

Your choices

  • To stop all analytics and purchase records from being sent, delete the app. That also deletes everything stored on your phone.
  • To ask about or request deletion of the anonymous analytics tied to your device, email us at the address below. We'll do our best to help, though since the data is anonymous we may need details from your phone to find it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
