Cute Reminders Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Cute Reminders is a small app made by Scott DiBenedetto, with art by Jo (@jojodrawss). This policy explains what the app keeps, what it sends, and why. Your reminder text and list names are stored on your device and are not sent to our analytics or purchase providers. The app sends limited usage and purchase information linked to device or app identifiers to help us understand which designs people use and support purchases.

Your reminders stay on your phone

Everything you type stays on your iPhone: list names, reminders, which items you've checked off, start and stop times, fonts and themes. It's saved on the device so the app and its home screen widget can show it. We don't have a server that receives it, and we can't see it.

Deleting the app may remove your locally stored reminders. Device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

No account, no ads, no cross-app advertising tracking

  • You don't need to sign up or log in.
  • There are no ads.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your information with data brokers or advertisers.

Device-linked usage analytics (Amplitude)

We use Amplitude to count how the app gets used, so we know which designs people love and what Jo should draw next. The app sends events like these:

  • a design was picked for a list, or which designs are on your lists that day
  • an unlock screen was opened
  • a design or the "every design" bundle was bought
  • restore purchases was tapped
  • a Cute Reminders widget is on the home screen

These events include the design's name, such as "chefcat", plus whether it was one of your free designs. They never include what you write in your reminders or list names.

Amplitude gives your device a random ID so events from the same phone can be counted together. It also receives basic technical details such as your device model, iOS version, app version and language. We've turned off IP address and city collection, and turned off Amplitude's automatic tracking, so only the events listed above are sent. We do not send your name, email address or Apple ID with these events. However, the device identifier lets events from the same device be connected over time. This is pseudonymous, device-linked information, not fully anonymous data.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

All payments go through Apple's App Store. We do not receive your payment card details or Apple Account credentials.

We use RevenueCat to keep a record of purchases, so we can see which designs sell and help if a purchase needs restoring. RevenueCat receives which products were bought and when, along with an app user identifier and basic device details. It doesn't receive your reminder text or list names. Purchase-completion events are also sent to Amplitude and associated with the device identifier. Device identifiers, product interactions and purchase history can therefore be linked to the same device or app user, as reflected in our App Store privacy disclosures.

Children’s privacy

Cute Reminders is intended for a general audience, rather than specifically for children under 13. If you have questions about a child’s information, please contact us at cutereminderssupport@gmail.com.

Your choices

  • Stopping use of the app stops new app usage events. There is currently no in-app analytics opt-out. Deleting the app may remove its locally stored reminders, but does not automatically delete records already held by Apple, Amplitude or RevenueCat.
  • To ask about or request deletion of analytics tied to your device, email us at the address below. We may need additional information to locate the device or app user identifier and verify your request. Some purchase records may need to be retained for purchase restoration or legal obligations. Apple manages its own purchase records.
